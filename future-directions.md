## 💫 Future Directions

### Computational Architecture & Machine Learning Pipelines
 - **Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) for Connectomics:** Utilizing GNNs to model how shifts in proliferative pathways affect macroscopic white matter tracts and structural connectivity. The goal is to predict how microscale alterations in specific neural crest lineages disrupt the ultimate Excitation/Inhibition (E/I) balance of cortical networks.
- **Epigenomic Analysis:** Using existing proprietary tools (e.g., Dr. Matteo Pellegrini’s Epigenetic Clock) and Developing pipelines to model and discover epigenetic biomarkers in ASD and WS. Beyond neurology, we should consider neuroimmunologic, neuroendocrine, and topological connectivity and lipid/solute transport phenomena in this epigenetic analysis.
- **Dimensionality Reduction & Multi-Disorder Clustering:** Rather than treating neurodevelopmental conditions as isolated diagnostic categories, we could use machine learning to analyze transcriptomic and clinical metadata across 200–300 distinct neurodevelopmental and psychiatric disorders. The objective is to re-cluster these conditions into shared, mechanistically defined biological "modules" or sub-phenotypes. 
- **Time-Series Signal Processing (FFT & Wavelet Transforms):** Analyzing continuous wavelet transforms (CWT) and Fast Fourier Transforms (FFT) to process multi-channel, non-linear biological signals in autism and Williams Syndrome. This includes mapping 2D EKG transforms (Heart Rate Variability) against quantitative EEG (brainwave) matrices to mathematically model autonomic, heart-brain phase-coupling as it relates to subjective social and empathy experiences.

### Conserved Evolutionary Genomics & Domestication Topologies

- **The Neural Crest Domestication Hypothesis:** Comparative genomic and epigenetic analysis tracking core loci associated with Williams Syndrome ($GTF2I$, $CLIP1$) across human hyper-social cohorts, domesticated canines (*Canis lupus familiaris*), and other domesticated species such as elephants. We seek to identify conserved DNA methylation signatures that act as evolutionary "volume knobs" for social tolerance and neural crest cell migration.
- **Endogenous Tryptamine & Motif Conservation:** Investigating conserved sequence motifs and expression pathways of Indolethylamine $N$-methyltransferase ($INMT$) across mammalian lineages. This includes examining historical anomalies in endogenous bufotenine production and tracing their overlap with structural variations found in Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD).
- **Morphogenetic Autonomic Biomarkers:** Exploring the structural topology of iris stellation via deep-learning computer vision models. Because the iris architecture is deeply tied to neural crest development and autonomic nervous system maturation, we propose investigating iris patterns as a non-invasive, structural biomarker for baseline sympathetic/parasympathetic tone and empathy questionnaire scoring.
