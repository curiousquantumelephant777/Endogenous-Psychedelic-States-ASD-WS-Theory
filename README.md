## Endogenous Empathogen Theory
This research investigates the 7.q11.23 locus (specifically *GTF2I*) as a significant regulator of sociobehavioral phenomena. I am mapping the divergence between *INMT* pathways (ASD) and *PEA/TAAR1* pathways (WS). I hope to create a topological model of consciousness where empathy becomes a quantifiable state of bio-electromagnetic coherence. 

# 💫 Core Frameworks
This research proposes a novel metabolic framework for understanding Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) and Williams Syndrome (WS) not as "deficits," but as distinct endogenous psychedelic-mimetic states. I hypothesize that the phenotypic mirroring observed between these conditions (social withdrawal and sensory intensity in ASD versus hypersociability and social fearlessness in WS) is driven by divergent shunts in methylation and trace amine pathways. This is further compounded by topological differences (e.g., in perineuronal net flux and lipid transport).

Specifically, this model proposes:
- ASD is driven by an overactive *INMT*/DMT/bufotenine pathway (leading to continual sensory overload and high signal-to-noise (SNR ratio), whereas WS is characterized by an overactive PEA/TAAR1 pathway (possibly involving novel endogenous substituted phenethylamines), which drives the hypersociability. I propose that this INMT pathway, if overloaded in ASD, shunts away from the Kyurensin serotonergic pathway.
-  Dysregulation in the Kyurensin pathway has been linked to immune system activation and potentially the accumulation of molecules that are toxic [1]. This could explain some of the neuroinflammation and methylation issues in ASD (e.g., microglia activation, MTHFR gene mutations seen in some cases of ASD). The proposed framework identifies the *ASMT-INMT* enzymatic axis as a primary biosynthetic pathway for endogenous empathogenic ligands, facilitating transcriptomic upregulation or altered feedback loops within this axis that induce recursive production feedback loops of methyl-substituted tryptamines (e.g., bufotenine), effectively shifting the sociocognitive manifold toward a permanent baseline state of sensory overload and high SNR with a rigid topological framework.
-  Building on *VMAT* research: *VMAT* is downregulated in ASD, and concurrently *TAAR1* may be upregulated, decreasing available trace amines in the autistic brain.

# 💗 Williams Syndrome and Heart-Centered Orientation
The **"Heart-Centered" orientation** in Williams Syndrome (open consititutive social state may be characterized by: 

- TAAR1-mediated surges in Serotonin and Oxytocin. This represents an socially open, empathogenic state with high fear extinction rates, where social trust is biologically unfettered. I hypothesize this is the phenotypic state of WS's social profile, which is also observed acutely after substituted (methylated) phenethylamine (most notably 2-CB and MDMA) administration. In this model, WS also represents a porous topology, a divergent mirror of ASD (lower PNN flux, looser connectivity in tissues due to *ELN* deletion, stellated irises).
- WS is characterized by the deletion of several genes on the *7.11.23* locus, including GTF2I, a gene known for its role in social connection. Conversely, a duplication of GTFT2I results in neuropsychiatric symptoms that mirror the social deficits seen in ASD. Interestingly, *GTF2I* (and *GTF2IRD1*) is thought to have played a role in the domestication of wolves to dogs [2]. I'm particularly interested in mapping the genomic lineage of these traits. I propose the overactive *INMT* in people with ASD may be a conserved gene from the *Incilius alvarius* toad lineage (which produces bufotenine (5-HO-DMT), a metabolite of 5-MeO-DMT.
