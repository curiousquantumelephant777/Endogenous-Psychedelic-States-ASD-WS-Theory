This repository documents the theoretical framework for endogenous biophysical states. Below are the peer-reviewed foundations supporting the INMT-shunt and phenethylamine-stellation models.

## I. Endogenous Tryptamine Research (The ASD/Indole Axis)

### [Dean, J. G., et al. (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-45812-y)
**"Biosynthesis and Extracellular Concentrations of N,N-dimethyltryptamine (DMT) in Mammalian Brain."**
* **Significance:** Provides empirical evidence for INMT expression in the cerebral cortex. This is the foundational proof for the "INMT-Shunt."

### [Szabo, A., et al. (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0106533)
**"The Endogenous Hallucinogen N,N-Dimethyltryptamine (DMT) Is a Sigma-1 Receptor Agonist..."**
* **Significance:** Links endogenous tryptamines to neuroprotection and antioxidant vigilance via Sigma-1 signaling.

### [Emanuele, E., et al. (2010)](https://www.nel.edu/userfiles/articlesnew/NEL310110A11.pdf)
**"Elevated urine levels of bufotenine in patients with autistic spectrum disorders and schizophrenia."**
* **Significance:** This study confirms the elevated presence of endogenous tryptamines (specifically Bufotenine) in individuals with ASD. This provides clinical grounding for the **INMT-Shunt** model, suggesting that neurodevelopmental variance is linked to a unique "Tryptamine Flux" within the cellular manifold.
